# M02 environment asset provenance

- `harbor-industrial-shack.glb`, `harbor-dock.glb`, and `harbor-watch-stand.glb` were converted from the user-supplied **3TD Studios Free Sample Pack #2**. Its included licence allows use in games and prohibits only repackaging/reselling the pack as original work.
- `ruin-building-01.glb` and `ruin-wreckage.glb` were converted from the user-supplied **LowPoly Apocalyptic Buildings** pack by Majadroid. Its included `INFO.txt` declares the package CC0; attribution is appreciated, not required.

The original archives remain in the user's Downloads folder. `tools/convert_m02_environment.py` records the deterministic Blender conversion used for these browser-ready GLB files.
